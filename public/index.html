Meet Anju

Anju Sharma Rijal · Educator

Anju Sharma Rijal is a licensed family child care provider with over 18 years of experience caring for over 100+ infants, toddlers, preschoolers, school-age children, and twins. She is passionate about creating a safe, nurturing, and engaging environment where every child is encouraged to learn, grow, and reach their full potential.

Before moving to the United States, Anju earned a Master's degree in Sociology and a Bachelor's degree in Arts and Humanities. She taught Social Studies and Arts to students in Grades 1–8 at a mission school in rural Nepal, bringing a strong educational foundation to her child care profession.

Known for her warmth, compassion, and family-centered approach, Anju fosters children's social, emotional, physical, and cognitive development through play-based learning and meaningful daily experiences. Families value her professionalism, patience, and commitment to providing high-quality care in a safe and welcoming environment.

We invite you to read our Parent Endorsements to learn how families' trust and support inspired Anju to establish Rijal Family Child Care, where she continues to serve children and families with dedication and care.

Financial Assistance (Voucher)

At Rijal Family Child Care, we believe every child deserves access to high-quality early education and care. We are proud to accept child care vouchers through Massachusetts-approved agencies, helping eligible families receive financial assistance for child care services.

If you are currently receiving child care assistance or would like to learn whether you qualify, we encourage you to contact your local Massachusetts Child Care Resource & Referral (CCR&R) agency or speak with us.

Our goal is to make high-quality, nurturing child care accessible and affordable for every family.

We proudly accept Massachusetts Child Care Financial Assistance (CCFA) vouchers through approved agencies.

Eligible families can use child care financial assistance through Massachusetts-approved voucher agencies. Contact us to learn more about enrollment and available openings.

Military Fee Assistance Program

We are approved for the Military/DoD Fee Assistance programs administered by Child Care Aware® of America (CCAoA). Our Provider ID number is #9239822.

Our child care facility meets the requirements for the fee assistance program and may receive fee assistance for military families that are approved as eligible to attend our facility. Military family eligibility can vary based on military branch and/or deployment status and cannot be determined until the family has applied to the program.

Please contact your Administration or Child Care Aware® of America (CCAoA) or speak with us.

We are happy to answer questions about the enrollment process and work with your voucher agency to make your transition as smooth as possible.
